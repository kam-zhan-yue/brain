# 表の空欄の推測

## 比例 (Proportion) - Medium
- Find some relation between two rows in a graph
- Use a calculator and smash until you find a multiplier that works

## 大小 (Size) - Hard
- The aim is finding relationships (what causes the number to go larger?)
- After finding the relationships, use the existing data to make a conjecture on the answer
- The answer is not clear, but you need to guess it can't be smaller than or larger than a number

## 算出式 (Calculation) - Hard
- The hints lie in the name of the rows
- Look at the units and try to multiply them if they make sense
- If you can't find the pattern, just move on

## 推移 (Transition) - Medium
- Find the pattern and calculate for the next 「?」
- If numbers are going up and down, likely it is multiply then divide sort of pattern

## 会計が同じ - Easy
- Look for totals that add up and remain the same over time
- E.g. Groups of people should be the same, items sold etc

## 積算 (Summing) - Easy
- Usually just multiplying two or more terms to get a value
- If there are two unknowns, subtract two sums to get the remainder

## 例ごと計算 - Easy
- Don't try to find individual values, combine and move things around
- Use algebra to find combinations


# Strategies
- If there are two rows with seemingly no pattern, divide and find a proportion
- If it is a huge table with seemingly no pattern, go with feeling
- Sometimes it really do be just dividing and finding a constant
- If there is a discount with no pattern, the discount might be applied in stages
