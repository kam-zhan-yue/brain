The idea is to stay nearby a station on the Toyoko Line so that I am effectively 20 mins from either Shibuya or Yokohama.

There are five stops
- Nakameguro Station
- Jiyugaoka Station
- Musashi-Kosugi Station
- Kihuna Station

## Requirements
- Bouldering Gym
- Running Track / Nature
- Cycling Access

### Nakameguro Station
- River for jogging
- Plenty of hip and trendy shops

- Supposedly good cycling and jogging spots by the river
- Can easily bike to neighbouring cities like Nakameguro
