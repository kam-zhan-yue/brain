Welcome everyone, to the Production Division Showcase.

For those who are new here and might not know about the Production Division. Let me tell you a little story.

This story started just over a little year ago, on the 15th of August. On that day, Jake and I ran the first ever Production Division induction, in front of around 40 members across three different teams. Each of these teams had a task. Make a game in a year.

What could possibly go wrong?

Jokes aside, development wasn't easy.

It was hard. For many of us, it was our first serious attempt at making a game. And it came with so many challenges. We extended deadlines, got busy with personal and university life, and struggled to complete the games.

Despite the challenges, you all pulled through and produced three amazing games.

The rhythm beat-em-up', TRACKLINE by Team Magma.
The narrative Zelda-like, Noelle's Ark by Team Aqua.
The Lovecraftian horror-cooking game, The Last Diner by Team Rocket.

Congratulations to everyone who worked on these games.

Now, I'd like to recap some statistics.

Originally, we had 39 members spread across three teams and the Media Division. During the journey, some of us have left, and others have joined our suffering. I'm happy to report that we've involved 54 people (according to Discord) and even introduced a new Quality Assurance team!

Let's look at how much we coded too.

- Merged 277 pull requests
- Closed 81 issues
- Made 1,922 commits

Not to mention the crazy amount of art, writing, and music that went into each of these games. You all made a monstrous effort with these games and I thank you from the bottom of my heart.

However, none of this would be possible without the hard work of a few special people. These are the people that worked tirelessly to organise your tasks. Who had to made the hardest decisions to keep the game on track. Who conducted your meetings and acted as a source of strength. I want to thank the Producers of each of these teams.

To Meryl, you stepped up into one of the most challenging roles in development and thrived. I remember you being hesitant at first when considering to take on the Producer role in the middle of development. I think that that was the best decision that was made for the team. Without your leadership, Team Rocket would not be where they are today. The game has drastically improved under your wing and I am happy to have seen you grow.

To Alex, you are the true unsung hero of Team Magma. Despite only being with the team for a few months, you rallied them up with incredible organisation, and got them back in sync. I remember being amazed at the Alpha Build and how far the game progressed in such a short time. Not only did you help as a Producer, you worked incredibly hard programming systems and delivering the best possible experience for players. It was a pleasure working alongside you.

Finally, to Zimo. Team Aqua has been through quite a lot, seeing many members leave for various personal reasons. Despite this, I don't think any team had connections quite as strong as Team Aqua. Everyone was excited to work on the game and brought all kinds of passion and opinions to meetings. I think this can be attributed to the wonderful environment you built for your team. Zimo isn't here today, but I want her team to know that I know the amount of effort she went to in order to make you all feel happy.

I'd like to ask the Producers to get on stage to say a few words each.

I have more words of thanks that I'd like to keep brief. To Thaha, thanks for doing so much ad-hoc work on the Media Division and pulling through as much as possible. To my QA team, I've been so impressed by your work with our automated builds and checklists. The games would barely be in a releasable state without you guys. To everyone, thank you for giving up your time over the past year. These games may not be perfect, but I wouldn't have traded this experience for anything else.

I've gotten to know and work with so many amazing people and build a lovely community. I know working with me can be stressful and I'm sorry if I've been harsh to anyone. But I hope it's been a great experience for everyone involved. If you're still around in the university or the club next year, I hope to see you guys again in the next iteration of the Production Division.
