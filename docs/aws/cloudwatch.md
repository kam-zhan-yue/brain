CloudWatch monitors AWS resources and the applications you run on AWS in real time, and offers many tools to give you system-wide observability of your application performance, operational health, and resource utilisation.

- Metrics collect and track key performance data at user-defined intervals
- Dashboards offer a unified view of your resources and applications with visualisations of your metrics and logs in a single location.
- Alarms can be set up to continuously monitor CloudWatch metrics against user-defined thresholds and can automatically alert you to breaches of the thresholds. Alarms can also trigger automated actions to respond to them.
