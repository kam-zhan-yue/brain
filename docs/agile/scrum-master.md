# Overview
There are two primary goals for the Scrum Master
- Ensures the Scrum Framework is followed by:
	- Guiding scrum events
	- Managing stand-ups and tracks progress
	- Administrating the scrum board
- Improves team workflows by:
	- Removing blockers
	- Fostering collaboration
	- Supporting team self-organisation

## Responsibilities
- Standups
	- Facilitates the standups / dķily scrums as needed
- Iteration / Sprint Planning Meetings
	- Protects the team from over-committing and scope creep
	- Aid in estimation and sub task creation
- Board Administration
	- Work as the administrator of the scrum board
	- Ensures that cards are up to date and the scrum tools are working well
- 1 on 1s
	- Meeting with team members and stakeholders as needed.
	- Iron out team disagreements about processes and work styles
	- 1 on 1s may not be needed, listen to your team
- Reporting
	- Regular analysis of burndown charts and other tools
	- Understand what gets build and at what cadence
- Blockers
	- Eliminates external blockers and manages internal roadblocks
	- Aims to improve workflows along the way
- Busy work
	- If the scrum team isn't functioning as well, it is the scrum master's problem
	- Might have to fix broken computers, move desks around, adjust temperatures, etc
	- Scrum masters should be comfortable doing anything

## Skills
- Leadership: Scrum Masters must exhibit leadership qualities without being authoritative. They serve the team and facilitate their self-organisation
- Effective communication: Ensures that all team members and stakehodlers understand the project's status, goals, and obstacles
- Empathy: It is essential to understand and emphathise with the team's challenges. Scrum masters need to support and motivate team members during difficulties
- Problem-Solving: Scrum masters must find solutions to deal with impediments and conflicts to keep the team on track
- Adaptability: Needs to adjust to the evolving needs to the team and the project
- Facilitation skills: The ability to effectively facilitate meetings and scrum ceremonies is vital. These events should run smoothly and focus on the intended outcomes
- Coaching and mentoring: Scrum masters coach and mentors team members in agile and scrum principles, helping them continually improve
- Technical knowledge: While not mandatory, having a technical background helps to better understand challenges faced by the team
- Time Management: Scrum masters must manage time effectively to keep the team on schedule
