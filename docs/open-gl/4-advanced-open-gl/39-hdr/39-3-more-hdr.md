The two tone mapping algorithms are a few of a large collection of tone mapping algorithms, each with their own strengths and weaknesses. Some tone mapping algorithms favour certain colours / intensities above others and some algorithms display both the low and high exposure colours at the same time to create colourful and detailed images.

There is also a collection of techniques known as automatic exposure adjustment or eye adaptation techniques that determine the brightness of the scene in the previous frame and slowly adapts the exposure parameter in such a way that the scene gets brighter in dark areas or darker in bright areas to mimic the human eye.

The real benefit of HDR rendering shows itself in large and complex scenes with heavy lighting algorithms.
