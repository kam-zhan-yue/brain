Lighting in the real world is complicated and dependent on many factors. OpenGL is based on approximations of reality using simplified models that are easier to process. These lighting models are based on the physics of light as we understand it.

## Phong Lighting
The major building blocks of the Phong lighting model consists of 3 components:
- Ambient Lighting: Even when it is dark, there is usually still some light somewhere in the world (the moon, a distant light), so objects are almost never completely dark. To simulate this, we use an ambient light that always gives the object some colour
- Diffuse Lighting: Simulates the directional impact a light object has on an object. The more a part of an object faces the light source, the brighter it becomes
- Specular: Simulates the bright spot of a light that appears on shiny objects. Specular highlights are more inclined to the colour of the light than the colour of the object.
