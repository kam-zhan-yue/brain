I'm currently covered by ITS Health Insurance (関東ITソフトウェア健康保険)

## [Insurance Benefits](https://www.its-kenpo.or.jp/hoken/kyufu/ichiran/index.html)
- 病気やケガをしたとき
	- 70% of expenses are covered
	- Dental is treated the same way
	- There is also a partial reimbursement. If your out-of-pocket expenses exceeds 20,000円, you will get paid it
- If you can't work because you're sick
	- Two-thirds of your standard daily wage for each day of absence
	- The first three consecutive days are a waiting period, then payment begins from the fourth day
- If I incur a [high expense treatment](https://www.its-kenpo.or.jp/hoken/kyufu/kougaku/kougakuiryou/index.html#kou02)
	- There is an annual limit based on your income bracket
	- It would be around 530,000円 on my current income
- If I'm [involved in an accident](https://www.its-kenpo.or.jp/hoken/kyufu/jiko/index.html)
	- If medical treatment is not provided by insurance, the insured perrson must promptly submit a notification to the insurer
	- Should contact the union's claim department immediately (当組合求償課)
- Does not provide any visits to bone-setting or osteopathic clinics (整骨院・接骨院)
- There are [brain MRI and lung CT](https://www.its-kenpo.or.jp/kanri/other/mr_ct/index.html) scans available for those above 40
	- MRI = 10,000円
	- CT = 5,000円
## Non-Health Benefits
- Apparently we can access well known [onsens and resorts for cheap](https://www.its-kenpo.or.jp/shisetsu/index.html)
- Around 5,000円 per night up to three nights
- Access to [restaurants for cheap](https://www.its-kenpo.or.jp/fuzoku/index.html)
