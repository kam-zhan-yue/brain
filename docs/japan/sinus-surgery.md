Endoscopic Sinus Surgery
- May remove nasal polyps and tumours, treat sinus infections, etc
- The doctor uses an endoscope to access and treat the problem through the nostrils

Septoplasty is a surgical procedure to straighten the nasal septum
Sinus Surgery clears blocked sinus passages, removes polyps, etc

## Doctor Visit
- I've had a blocked nose all my life and have had trouble breathing
- I usually breathe through my mouth at night when sleeping
- In Malaysia, I saw a doctor who put a camera in my nose and showed that the canal was extremely small
- I want to know what the problem is so that I can solve it and if I need surgery or not

手術が必要かどうかも知りたいです
