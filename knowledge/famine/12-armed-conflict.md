## Armed Conflict
- There are three main categories of armed conflict:
1. Minor armed conflict, less than 1,000 battle deaths
2. Intermediate armed conflict, more than 1,000 battle deaths throughout the course of conflict, but less than 1,000 in any given year
3. War, more than 1,000 battle deaths in a year
- In 2016, there were 49 armed conflicts

## Armed Conflict and Famine
- Majority of people who are food insecure live in countries affected by armed conflict, 489-815 million malnourished people
- Progress in reducing hunger is slowest in countries affected by armed conflict
- Direct: deliberate denial of food to opponents and perceived supporters of opponents
- Indirect: diverts public resources, high ratio of spending on arms relative to social services
- Direct and Indirect: land mines deny access to arable land and transport services

## Complex Emergencies
- Drive humanitarian crises, where there is total or considerable breakdown of authority resulting from internal or external conflict and which requires an international response that goes beyond the mandate or capacity of any single agency
- Complex emergencies consist of:
	- extensive violence and mortality
	- massive displacements of people
	- widespread social and economic change
	- weak or non-existent government
	- require large scale
	- multi-faceted international assistance
- Always have an associated food criss and are multi casual
	- Causes can't be understood independent of temporal and social context
	- Unsuccessful interventions can exacerbate problems
## Refugees
- A person who 'owing to a well-founded fear or being persecuted for reasons of race, religion, nationality, membership in a particular social group, or political opinion, is outside the country of his / her nationality and is unable to or, owing to such feat, is unwilling to avail her / himself of the. protection of that country'
- People relocate up to 6 times in response to famine and / or war. 80% of these refugees are women and children because women are particularly vulnerable to wars, famines, and new locations due to a lack of access and social support networks
- Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs) move within countries, not under UNHCR mandate, but with the same set of problems as refugees
