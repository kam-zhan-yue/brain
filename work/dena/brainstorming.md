AI時代のプロダクト・サービス

When someone brings up the 'age of AI' to me, the first thing that I can think about is the deterioration of our intelligence, the environments, and our Internet. In the age of AI, I think it is vital to make something that protects these things. However, the aim of the theme is to solve a particular pain point or to satisfy the needs of either us, our friends, our family, or our colleagues.

In regards to the people around us and how they use AI, I imagine that they are unfamiliar or haven't heard of the use of AI agents. I don't really see the value of chatbots, but I do see the value of AI agents. The fact that they can absorb huge chunks of information and summarise / act on it saves us a bunch of time through cognitive offloading. Having a human-in-the-loop also helps to ensure that nothing occurs without our permission. However, there are still plenty of security risks that surround that type of software and that we should still be aware of.

I think a lot of people are also unaware of how detrimental AI can be to the environment and to creatives. Maybe something that helps them be aware of this to make more intelligent decisions?

## Concerns over AI Usage
I hold plenty of concerns over AI usage, to the point where I avoid using any AI tools in my everyday life. However, I feel like more people need to be aware of the risks as well as the benefits of using AI.
- News Related: A lot of people consume information through the news. They are vulnerable to getting misinformed. Something similar to Ground News but for Japan? Where you can see people's real opinions on this and how left / right leaning it is?
- What tools are safe, what tools are not safe. An online index to check AI tools. I feel like this exists though.
## Lack of Experience with AI
Lots of people also have no experience with hooking up agents or using them (like me). Having some user-friendly interface to do that would be good, but I imagine this is a solved problem and I just haven't looked.

But it is undeniable that people are always hearing AI has productivity gains, even though the largest productivity gains have been in programming and the flow-on effects haven't reached other sectors just yet.

## Looking for Pain Points
The most common use of AI is still chatbots. People still constantly interact with chatbots and I always see people on the train just staring at their screens while waiting for their response. Is this a problem? Is there something wrong there?

Another pain point is just how expensive and slow it is. Larger models take minutes to reason and the token costs can skyrocket through the roof.
## Looking for Opportunities
AI advancements promise to give people back their time. Instead, we are seeing people using AI succumb to overworking and stress. AI fatigue is a real problem and there is no clear solution other than to have stricter guidelines when using it.

At the end of the day. I want to make a game. With things becoming more AI integrated, and social media being flooded with bots and AI, it is hard to find something that is real. I also want to get to know certain people better. Maybe it's better to make an internal team-building game? A multiplayer game where the goal is working together to solve things. 

The problem is real too. 
- People are talking more to chatbots than their partners
- Employees work more with AI than other people
Sometimes, we need something to remind us that the people around us are real. And this is in line with delight too! Helping remote teams conduct team building exercises online.
