## ライフプラニング
Generally, there is something called 社会保険. This is separated into:
- 健康保険 health insurance
- 厚生年金保険 welfare annuity insurance
- 雇用保険 unemployment insurance
- 労災保険 worker's accident insurance
These are insurance that kick into action when you are unable to work for a variety of reasons.

In Japan, the number of pensioners is increasing while the population growth is decreasing. The necessity for pension contributions have never been higher.
## DC/DB制度の概要
In Japan, there are three levels of pension contributions.
- 国民年金: Everyone from the ages of 20-65 pays this.
- 厚生年金保険: 
- 企業年金: DC/DB is part of this.
Participation means more contributions to the pension

The contributions to DC/DB are not subject to tax! However, it is taken from your income. This is like your superannuation.

DC takes instalments, you invest it, then you can access this at 60 years of age.
DB takes instalments, it automatically is invested, then you can access it after 60 years or retirement. The investment performs at a fixed rate and you don't have to do anything. You can take it out when retiring or taking a break from employment.
