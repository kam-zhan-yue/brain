## Logbooks Follow Up
Will says that the `url` returned by the digital logbook is only a limited access view. Since we are attaching the pdf to the email anyways, it makes sense to store it somewhere in S3.

Then, the resulting `url` will exist.

### How are the PDFs actually attached to the email?
`IssuanceEmail._add_attachment` will attach a pdf to the email as follows:
```python
def _add_attachments(self, msg, attachments):  # type: ignore[no-untyped-def]
	for attachment in attachments:
		if any(attachment["path"].startswith(p) for p in ["https://", "http://"]):
			try:
				f = request.urlopen(attachment["path"])  # noqa: S310
			except Exception as e:
				raise Exception(
					f"Failed to prepare attachment {attachment['path']}."
					f" {type(e).__name__}: {str(e)}"
				)
			msg.attach(attachment["name"], f.read(), attachment["type"])
		else:
			try:
				with default_storage.open(attachment["path"]) as f:
					msg.attach(attachment["name"], f.read(), attachment["type"])
			except FileNotFoundError as e:
				raise EmailPreparationFailure(str(e))
```

Hence, what we could do is open the path when we process the logbook, then we generate it in S3! This could be useful for the attach document acceptance test, too.
