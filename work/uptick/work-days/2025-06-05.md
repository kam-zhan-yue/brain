### BG Task Fixture
At this point in time, we don't have a way to properly gauge the status of child BG Tasks. Hence, to get over the hurdle, we can introduce a way to wait for the requested BGTask through a fixture instead.

Ideally, we would want the UI to show the status of the task (i.e. sending emails) but that would require many structural changes.
