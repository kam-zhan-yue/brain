## Evie
The eponymous protagonist. Evie is a tabby cat who has grow slightly chubby from her days staying at home and doing no other exercise than annoying her owner. The outside world is completely unknown to her, so everything is completely new and frightening. However, she has enough courage to venture into the unknown to find her beloved brother, Umbra.

Her leading clue to finding Umbra is a photo that her owner took of where they were found. She must go around and collect these photos, as she is sure Umbra must be there.
## Umbra
Evie's adventurous brother. Umbra is a black cat whose years of adventuring has built him to be slimmer and stronger. Although Evie and Umbra came from the same litter, Umbra acted as Evie's older brother, constantly teaching her new things and protecting her from the dangers of the outside world.
