### Summary
A semi open-world game filled with minigames and oddball characters, teaching the player about coping with loss.

Evie is a housecat left at home while her owner is out on a trip. Her brother, Umbra, is known for being particularly adventurous and often leaves the house for days at a time. However, he hasn't been back for a week and Evie has become worried sick. Hence, Evie sets out on her first grand adventure into the unknown to find Umbra, encountering larger-than-life characters and using their advice to find her lost brother.

### Gameplay
Evie is a regular housecat who can do everything a cat can do. Evie can jump to high ledges, pounce between platforms, and sprint to shelter. Evie's paws are notorious for touching things, bringing life to the objects around her.

As Evie ventures around the neighbourhood, she meets oddballs who have met Umbra before. In order to gain their trust and extract information, she must complete quests for them. These include minigames, fetch quests, or going to different locations.
