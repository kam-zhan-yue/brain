### Game Mechanics
- A game like Phoenix Wright where you have to defend or prosecute Santa
- Phoenix Wright has to point out contradictions in the lies. Interacts with evidence and interjects mid-testimony in order to show contradictions
- In our case, we should be defending Santa.
	- Hand-written notes from children
- Pressing the witness at every possible statement -> the witness will slip up and lie
- Ok this only works because Phoenix Wright knows his defendant is innocent.
- In our case, Santa really did commit all these crimes. So we want to be putting him away.
- We will present evidence to take him down
- What do I ask him about? (choices to ask the witness)
	- Bell boy checked in April May, but missed the fact that April May had another person
	- Purposely withheld information until you question for it

### Story
- What crimes can Santa be held up for?
	- Mistreating / underpaying elves
	- Mistreating Mrs. Claus
	- Breaking and Entry into Homes -> Santa has never been seen before

### Law Concepts
- Badgering: When the lawyer repeatedly asks the witness the same question or using overly aggressive or hostile questioning techniques during cross-examination. The goal of the lawyer is not to elicit information but rather to harass, intimidate, or argue with the witness
